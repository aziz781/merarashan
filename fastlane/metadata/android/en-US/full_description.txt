Mera Rashan is the easiest way for Pakistani families to track their ration card, BISP payments, and monthly subsidy entitlements — all in one secure app.

Whether you're checking your atta quota, reviewing past transactions, or downloading a monthly statement for your records, Mera Rashan brings everything together in a clean, fast, mobile-first experience.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
WHAT YOU CAN DO
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

✓ VIEW YOUR RATION CARDS
See all your registered ration cards, holder details, family members, and entitlements at a glance.

✓ TRACK TRANSACTIONS
Browse your full transaction history for atta, ghee, flour and other subsidised items. Filter by month and year to find exactly what you need.

✓ MONTHLY STATEMENTS
Download and share PDF statements for any month — perfect for personal records or sharing with family.

✓ INSTANT PUSH NOTIFICATIONS
Get notified the moment a new transaction is recorded against your card. Never miss an update.

✓ IN-APP NOTIFICATION INBOX
Review all your past alerts in one place. Swipe to dismiss, tap to open the related card or transaction.

✓ SECURE OTP LOGIN
No passwords to remember. Sign in safely with a one-time code sent to your phone.

✓ LIGHT & DARK THEME
Automatically matches your system theme, or pick the look you prefer.

✓ WHATSAPP SUPPORT
Need help? Reach our support team directly on WhatsApp from inside the app.
